How MedFlow estimates time

Remaining days = unfinished scheduled workload ÷ your available study minutes per day, rounded up. Per-subject estimates use that subject's unfinished minutes and the daily capacity you set. These are planning estimates, not promises; update task duration and your real pace as you learn.
